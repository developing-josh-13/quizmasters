How to Play

Selecting a Quiz

Use the buttons in the navigation bar at the top of the page to select your chosen quiz topic. The available topics are Food and Drink, Maths, Music, Sports and Technology.

Playing a Quiz

Each quiz has 5 questions on the selected topic, with a choice of 4 possible answers. You may only select 1 answer and only 1 answer will be correct. Once you have chosen your answer, click the Next Question button to move forward.

Post-Quiz Results

On the last question, once you are satisfied with your answers, click the Submit Answers button. This will take you to the post-quiz results screen where your total score is displayed.
